    From the sky to your seat

    One continuous shot.

    1. Pick a standHover the bowl and every stand lights up with its price and the seats left.
    2. Pick a blockThe camera flies down to it. Booked seats turn grey, the rest are yours.
    3. Pick your seatThe seats around it go grey one by one, and the ticket shows the real view from your seat.
    4. Upgrade to the boxStep into a skybox, pick a leather armchair and see the pitch from there.
    Live 3D · 48,000 seats

    Every stadium and cinema is designed around sightlines, the line from each seat to the stage. Sightline renders yours. 48,000 seats, each with its own view, so the ticket you buy is the seat you wanted.
